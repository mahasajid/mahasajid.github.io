Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant — Virginia Tech

Aug 2023 – Present · Advisors: Brendan David-John and Bo Ji

Collective Situational Awareness in Asymmetric MR. Led an end-to-end study of how communication modality shapes collective situational awareness in asymmetric MR, including a networked HoloLens 2 experiment, a 12-participant pilot, and an IRB-approved counterbalanced study with 48 participants across 24 dyads. Developed team-level awareness measures and multimodal analysis pipelines integrating gaze, speech, SAGAT, workload, and performance. Manuscript under review at IEEE VR 2027.

Security Attacks in Remote Collaborative MR. Designed and conducted a mixed-methods security study with two pilot studies and a 20-participant dyadic main study. Engineered four real-time attacks in a networked Unity/HoloLens 2 system, including click-redirection and MR-specific occlusion attacks. The work resulted in a first-author IEEE VR 2025 paper and was featured in Computerworld.

Bystander Privacy Indicators in XR. Collaborated on an iterative mixed-methods study using focus groups to develop visual and multimodal privacy-indicator concepts, followed by a usability and contextual-usefulness evaluation. The work resulted in an IEEE ISMAR Adjunct 2025 publication.

Undergraduate Research Assistant — LUMS

Jan 2022 – Feb 2023 · Advisors: Fareed Zaffar and Murtaza Taj

Developed a satellite-imagery analysis pipeline that achieved 92% accuracy in identifying constructed areas, together with a GIS-integrated decision-support platform for detecting land-use change and supporting property-tax verification. The work contributed to the International Growth Centre technical report Cross Verification of Revenue Using Remote Sensing Data.
