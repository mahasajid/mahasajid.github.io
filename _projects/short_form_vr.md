**September 2023 – December 2023**

This project examined short-form content addiction in virtual reality through the lens of **Flow Theory**. I identified a research gap around the rise of mixed-reality platforms and studied how immersive delivery may shape engagement with short-form content.

I conducted a **within-subjects experimental study** and analyzed quantitative data using paired-samples statistical testing. The project contributed design recommendations intended to reduce addictive interaction patterns as social-media experiences move into immersive platforms.

**Areas:** Human-Computer Interaction · Social Computing · Virtual Reality · Experimental Design · Quantitative Analysis
