**September 2020 – December 2020**

Designed a mobile application to make information about **women's health, law, education, and harassment** more accessible to women from varied socioeconomic backgrounds who had access to a smartphone.

Because the target audience included less tech-savvy and older users, the project emphasized **intuitive, learnable UX design**. The application was designed for Urdu support and voice-assisted interaction, with information presented through both readable/listenable articles and trivia-based learning activities.

**Areas:** UX/UI Design · Human-Computer Interaction · Mobile App Development · Accessibility · Social Impact
