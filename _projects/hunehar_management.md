**January 2021 – April 2021**

Developed a management system for **Hunehar Welfare School** in Islamabad, Pakistan, where students are supported by sponsors around the world. The system was designed to simplify communication, sponsorship tracking, payment management, and onboarding for the school administration.

Sponsors could view a sponsored child's academic progress and communicate through letters, while administrators could manage sponsor records, student records, payment status, and onboarding workflows through a centralized web application.

**Areas:** Database Systems · Software Engineering · Web Development · UX/UI Design · Human-Computer Interaction
