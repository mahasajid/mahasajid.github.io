I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at **Virginia Tech**. My research lies at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction, Extended Reality, and Security & Privacy**. I study how people perceive, communicate, and respond to security and privacy risks in collaborative immersive environments, with a particular focus on **collaborative XR** and **collective situation awareness**.

My work combines controlled user studies, mixed-methods research, interactive system development, and quantitative and qualitative analysis to understand human behavior in emerging computing environments and inform the design of safer, more usable immersive systems.

### Research interests

**Human-Computer Interaction · Extended Reality · Collaborative XR · Security & Privacy · Situation Awareness**

My current research examines three connected questions: how collaborators recognize and respond to attacks in shared immersive environments, how communication modalities shape collective situation awareness, and how XR systems can communicate privacy-relevant information to bystanders through accessible multimodal cues.

I am especially interested in research that connects rigorous empirical study with the design of interactive systems, translating observations about human behavior into practical guidance for safer and more effective collaborative technologies.
