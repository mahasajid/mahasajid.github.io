## Research Experience

### Graduate Research Assistant
**Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA** — *Aug 2023 – Present*  
Advisors: Dr. Brendan David-John and Dr. Bo Ji

#### Collective Situational Awareness in Asymmetric MR

- Led an end-to-end investigation of how communication modality shapes collective situational awareness in asymmetric MR, designing and implementing a networked HoloLens 2 experiment, refining the protocol through a 12-participant pilot, and conducting an IRB-approved, counterbalanced study with 48 participants across 24 dyads.
- Extended SAGAT-based collective situational awareness assessment by introducing two team-level measures—information coverage and accurate alignment—alongside established correctness and response-alignment measures, showing that aggregate scores can obscure meaningful differences in how knowledge is distributed and shared across teams.
- Built multimodal analysis pipelines integrating gaze, speech, SAGAT, workload, and performance data, deriving task-space gaze and verbal-coordination measures to characterize how teams distributed attention and coordinated across conditions.
- Found that Gaze+Voice improved task performance and shared awareness, while Gaze-only produced the strongest spatial gaze convergence, showing that performance, co-attention, and collective awareness capture distinct aspects of collaboration. Manuscript is under review at IEEE VR 2027.

#### Security Attacks in Remote Collaborative MR

- Designed and executed a mixed-methods security study on how users recognize and respond to attacks in remote collaborative MR, iteratively refining the task, attack conditions, and study protocol across two pilot studies with 16 participants before conducting an IRB-approved main study with 20 participants across 10 dyads.
- Engineered four real-time security attack interventions in a networked Unity-based HoloLens 2 platform, including a novel click-redirection mechanism that manipulated 3D object interactions and MR-specific occlusion attacks that exploited invisible spatial elements.
- Triangulated first-person recordings, behavioral observations, questionnaires, and semi-structured interviews to uncover gaps between users’ perceived and actual security behavior; 54% reported no attack during spatial occlusion, while 67% failed to report the mitigation strategy they had actually used.
- Translated findings on attack misattribution and mitigation into design recommendations for immersive security indicators, interaction transparency, and user training; resulted in a first-author IEEE VR 2025 paper featured in Computerworld.

#### Bystander Privacy Indicators in Extended Reality (XR)

- Collaborated on an iterative mixed-methods study of privacy indicators for situationally impaired XR bystanders, using four focus groups with 8 participants to develop five visual and multimodal concepts followed by a 7-participant evaluation of usability and contextual usefulness.
- Analyzed qualitative and quantitative feedback across indicator designs, finding that visual-only cues were less effective under situational impairment while multimodal indicators were preferred in privacy-sensitive scenarios; the work resulted in an IEEE ISMAR Adjunct 2025 publication.
